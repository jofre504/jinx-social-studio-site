Make space for your next idea.

JINX Social Studio is a private, locally hosted workspace designed to help authorized creators connect their social accounts and, as development continues, organize content, prepare posts, and manage a publishing workflow.

Current status: The current v0.2 integration supports account connection and basic account identification. Video editing, automated publishing, and analytics are planned and are not active yet.
Secure connections

Connect supported social accounts using the platforms' own authorization pages. JINX does not request social account passwords.

Private workspace

The JINX application is hosted locally and restricted to authorized members. This public site only explains the service and its policies.

Creator roadmap

Media library, editing, scheduling, publishing, and analytics are planned features that will be released and tested separately.

Information for account owners

Before connecting an account, review how JINX handles account information and your access to third-party services.
