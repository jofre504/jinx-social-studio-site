Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the JINX Social Studio operator handles information when an authorized user connects a social media account through the current v0.2 application.

1. What information the app handles

  • JINX account identity: The member account associated with each social connection.
  • Connected account details: The social platform, account ID, channel or display name, connection time, granted scopes, and token expiration information.
  • Authorization credentials: OAuth access tokens and, when issued by the provider, refresh tokens needed to maintain an authorized connection.
  • Connection and error information: Operational data needed to complete sign-in and diagnose connection failures may appear in service logs.

The current integration requests YouTube read-only channel access, TikTok's user.info.basic, and Instagram's basic business-account information permission, depending on which provider is connected. Additional content publishing and analytics permissions are not active in v0.2.

2. Why this information is used

The app uses the information to securely associate a connected social account with the correct authorized JINX member, show which account is connected, and support disconnection or reconnection.

3. Where information is stored

The current social connection records and OAuth tokens are stored on the JINX host computer in locally encrypted storage using AES-256-GCM. Developer credentials are kept in a separate local configuration file. Encryption helps protect stored records, but the host operator and processes with sufficient local access must still protect the computer, encryption key, and backups. This public website does not host social access tokens or the private JINX database.

4. When information is shared

Connecting an account necessarily involves communication with the selected platform's login and API servers—for example, Google/YouTube, TikTok, or Instagram/Meta. JINX sends authorization requests and verifies the linked account using each provider's official API. This public information website is hosted through GitHub Pages; GitHub may process visitor requests under its own policies. We do not intentionally embed third-party advertising or analytics scripts on these informational pages.

5. Control, retention, and deletion

Social connection records currently remain stored until a member disconnects that account or the operator deletes the stored information. Disconnecting removes the corresponding active local connection record. Disconnecting does not automatically revoke a token at the provider, and separate provider-side revocation may be necessary. Local host backups and diagnostic logs, if configured, may need separate deletion. There is not yet an automated time-based retention policy for these connection records.

6. Security

JINX restricts social connection requests to authenticated member sessions and associates records with individual JINX member IDs. A short-lived, single-use authorization state helps validate OAuth callbacks. The JINX workspace remains privately hosted; the public pages linked here are informational only. No internet-connected service or local device is completely risk-free.

7. Planned features

Video uploads, post scheduling, automatic publishing, editing, and analytics are future features. This policy will require updates before those features are activated, to explain any new data collected, retention periods, and third-party access.

8. Contact and requests

To ask about, correct, or request deletion of information held by the JINX operator, contact: jinxsocialstudio@gmail.com. You may also disconnect social accounts within your JINX workspace and revoke access from the linked provider.

9. Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy as Social Studio features or data practices change. The updated date above will reflect revisions, and authorized users should be informed about material changes.
